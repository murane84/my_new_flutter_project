How each element earns its place

🎧Sorted by presence, not recency

"Listening now" rises to the top. The organizing principle is who you can be with right now — one tap into their song. This is the roadmap's ★ "Now-Playing presence → join in one gesture," made the spine of the whole page rather than a badge.

💿The disc is the identity

Story ring (outer) + real-track-position arc (mid) + record (inner). It spins and shows the arc only while actually playing, so motion always means something. One avatar = story · playing · person, no separate stories strip.

💞Our Space leads (the wedge)

The pinned pair with a "close since / days in a song" counter is the long-distance-couple wedge and the Together plan made visible — shared, felt, sticky. It's the first thing the eye lands on because closeness is the product.

🎁Moments, quietly present

The small gift glyph on a row is a song dedication / "thinking of you" (Lever 2 — spikes). Emotional, one-off, on-thesis — never a banner, just an affordance where a moment naturally belongs.

🔀Taste-match as discovery

"82% taste match · you both love Goon" replaces a dead "Contact" label with a reason to reach out — the music-graph moat, surfaced where it creates a spark instead of buried in a separate tab.

🔒The intimate surface stays clean

The hard rule made visible: no ad ever inside a room, call, or thread. Ads live only in a clearly-public "discover" space. On desktop the right pane is the intimate surface — so it's quiet by design.

Functionality · responsiveness · what to build behind it

One contextual line per row, prioritized: live track → typing… → unread preview → taste-match → last-seen. Never stack them. Join is the only persistent CTA — call/message/dedicate/remove live in swipe (mobile) and row-hover (desktop). Empty state matters more now that the list is scoped to real friends: a quiet circle should feel inviting ("Add friends · scan contacts · find your taste-twins"), never broken.

Reflow, don't stretch: the phone's stacked zones become rail + presence-pane on desktop — the list collapses left, and the right pane hosts the room preview and the "now playing wall" (Concept 02's mosaic, finally in its right home as a mode, not the primary list). Hover reveals actions; touch uses swipe.

Motion budget: only on-screen and currently-playing discs animate; off-screen discs freeze; one shared ticker; prefers-reduced-motion respected. The arc is bound to the real playback position from the audio handler — true, not decorative.

The enabler (and the bridge to C1): "Listening now" needs a live now-playing presence broadcast over the WebSocket (expiring "user X is playing track Y"), plus the roadmap's non-negotiables underneath it — authenticate the main socket (A1), WS-first delivery (A3), and P2P Listen-Together (A4). Build that presence layer once and it powers this friend list and Listening Rooms. This surface is C1's front door.
