Aluta — four ways to rebuild the friend list

Same contacts, same palette, four different structures. The thing that makes the current page read as WhatsApp is the arrangement, not the colour — so each of these changes what the list is sorted by and what a row is made of.

Concept 01

Tuned In

Sorted by who's playing music, not by who messaged last. Live listeners rise to the top as wide cards you can join in one tap. Everyone else sits quiet below.

Concept 02

The Mosaic

No rows at all. Each person is a square tile carrying their current or last-played cover art. Reads as a music library made of people. Boldest option — hardest to scan past ~30 contacts.

Concept 03

Ringed rows

The stories strip is absorbed into the avatars — gradient ring means an unseen story. Tap ring for story, tap name for chat. Buys back a full screenful and drops the most Instagram-ish element.
