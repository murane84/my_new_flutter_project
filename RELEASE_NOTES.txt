Your 1:1 text messages are now end-to-end encrypted: only you and your friend can read them, not even Aluta's server.
Secret hearts and timed hearts (and Dedicate from the music player) are encrypted the same way.
Our Space words are encrypted for the people in the Space: moments, diary, notes, memos, kept hearts and kept statuses.
Listen Together chat lines and GIF reactions are encrypted for the room.
Link a computer with the QR code and your encrypted chat history comes with it.
An encrypted chat never goes back to unencrypted: if your friend isn't signed in anywhere, Aluta asks you to try again later.
Replying from a notification is encrypted too.
Search now finds words in your encrypted chats (searched on your own device).
Delete for me, delete for everyone and clear chat also remove the words from your device.
On the web, encrypted chats work in one tab at a time: click a tab to use it there.
Photos, voice notes, songs and group chats aren't encrypted yet; they're coming next.
