Aluta is in active development. This page describes what the current build can do today; features are added and refined often, so what you see in the app may be a little ahead of, or behind, this page. For the rules around using these features, and more on how they work, see the Terms of Use.

What Aluta is

Aluta is a social app that brings your conversations and your music into one place. It combines private and group chat, voice and group calling, ephemeral Stories, and a built-in music player with a shared “Listen Together” mode — so talking to friends and sharing what you're listening to happen side by side, not in separate apps. It runs on Android, Windows desktop, and in the browser.

What you can do today

Chat & media

One-to-one and group chats with photos, files, voice notes and GIFs. Reactions, replies, edit and delete-for-everyone, pinned messages, typing indicators, and delivered/read receipts with “seen by” in groups. You can also share your location and contact cards. Messages are sent over encrypted connections but are not end-to-end encrypted in this beta.

Voice & group calls

Peer-to-peer voice calls, one-to-one and in groups, that are never recorded. Calls can be answered and hung up from Bluetooth and car head units, just like a normal phone call.

Stories / Status new

Post a photo, a short video, a text card, or a “now playing” music moment that disappears after about 24 hours. Friends' stories appear in a tray above your chats with a coloured ring while unseen, and you can see who viewed yours.

Music player & Listen Together

Play the music on your device with full lock-screen, Bluetooth and car controls. Tidy up song details (title, artist, and more) and have those edits follow your account. Identify a song that's playing around you, share a track into a chat, and start a “Listen Together” session to play in sync with a friend.

Presence & friends

Online and last-seen presence, a friends list you can build from your contacts (with your permission), and status rings that show at a glance who has something new to share.

Your account & devices

Sign up with an email, add an optional phone number for one-tap calls and a profile picture. Protect your account with two-factor authentication, and recover it by email or authenticator code. Link a computer by QR — WhatsApp- Web style — and sign linked devices out remotely. On phones, an optional fingerprint/face lock keeps the app private, handled entirely on-device.

Notifications

Push notifications wake your device for new messages and incoming calls even when the app is closed, and tapping one takes you straight to the right chat or group.

Where Aluta is headed

Aluta is being built in stages. Recent work has focused on Stories, richer call controls, and tighter music sharing; upcoming stages explore shared “listening rooms” and more ways to enjoy music together. Because it's a beta, expect rough edges and occasional changes — your feedback shapes what comes next.
